# AI-GovPort
Turning business logic and SQL into structural guardrails for enterprise AI.

Welcome to my AI Governance & Model Risk Portfolio! 

Hey there! Thanks for stopping by.

If you've spent any time playing around with modern generative AI, you’ve probably noticed that it can be incredibly confident, even when it is completely making things up. While that's annoying when you're looking up trivia, it's an absolute nightmare for a multi-million dollar corporation trying to make financial or strategic decisions.

My background sits right at the intersection of a BCom in Business & Marketing Management and practical data manipulation via SQL. Because of that, my brain is naturally wired to look past the smooth, fancy language of AI models and actively poke holes in their business logic.

What this repository is all about:
This space isn’t about building advanced neural networks from scratch. It’s about auditing them.
I take real-world AI logic flaws, map them out using global enterprise safety frameworks (like the NIST AI RMF), and show the actual financial and operational damage a company faces if an executive blindly trusts an unverified model.

Inside, you'll find:
Case Studies: Real breakdowns of AI models hallucinating strategic mandates, missing core context, or failing behavioral safety tests.
Business Impact Analysis: Translating technical glitches into clear risks regarding corporate capital allocation and legal liability.
The Technical Fixes: Practical prompt guardrails, human-in-the-loop triggers, and SQL data-lineage queries to prevent these errors before they happen.

Feel free to browse through the folders or check out the code. If you're a Chief Risk Officer, a Model Validator, or just someone interested in deploying AI safely within a business, I'd love to connect!
